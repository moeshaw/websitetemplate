About

I am a computer science professional pursuing a Master of Science in Computer Science at the University of Michigan–Flint, with an academic background in software engineering. My experience spans software development, data analysis, research, and application prototyping.

My research experience includes analyzing more than 200 metabolites to investigate potential indicators of Parkinson's disease using Python and scientific computing libraries. I have also contributed to software projects involving educational tools, mobile applications, and computer vision.

I am interested in applying machine learning, data science, and analytical problem-solving to real-world challenges. I am also expanding my skills in iOS development and design thinking through the Apple Foundation Program.

My goal is to continue developing my technical and analytical skills while contributing to collaborative projects that combine software engineering, research, and data-driven problem-solving.
